About the Author

The author of *Metal Gear Solid 3: Snake Eater* is Hideo Kojima, a renowned video game designer and director. Known for his innovative storytelling and complex narratives, Kojima has created a legacy within the gaming industry. He often explores themes of war, identity, and the human condition, with *Metal Gear Solid 3* serving as a quintessential example of his artistry. This installment is celebrated for its rich character development, deep philosophical undertones, and the seamless integration of gameplay and storytelling.
